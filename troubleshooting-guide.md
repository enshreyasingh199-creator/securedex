# Troubleshooting Guide

This guide explains how to solve common securedex problems.


## Unable to sign in

### Problem

You cannot sign in to your securedex account.

### Possible causes

- Incorrect email address or password.
- Account is locked.
- Internet connection is unavailable.

### Solution

1. Check that your email address is correct.
2. Re-enter your password.
3. Check your internet connection.
4. If you still cannot sign in, select **Forgot Password**.


## Unable to connect to the network.

### Problem

securedex cannot connect to your network.

### Possible causes

- Network Connection is unavailable.
- Firewall is blocking securedex.
- Incorrect Network Configuration.

### Solution

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Verify that securedex is allowed through your firewall.
3. Check your network configuration.
4. Restart securedex.
5. Try connecting again.

if the problem continues, contact your network administrator.
